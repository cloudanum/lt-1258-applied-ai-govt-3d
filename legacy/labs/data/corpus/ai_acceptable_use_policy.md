# Generative AI Acceptable Use Policy (Training Excerpt)

*Simplified, synthetic training document modeled on emerging federal AI acceptable-use guidance. Course labs only.*

## Scope

This policy governs employee and contractor use of generative AI tools, including public chat assistants and agency-provisioned enterprise assistants, for official work.

## Permitted Uses

Staff may use approved generative AI tools to draft routine correspondence, summarize public documents, brainstorm options, generate diagrams, and get help understanding or writing code. All AI output must be reviewed by a human before it is used in an official product.

## Prohibited Uses

Staff must not enter nonpublic information into any AI tool that has not been approved for that data category. Prohibited inputs include personally identifiable information, procurement-sensitive information, law-enforcement-sensitive information, and any classified or controlled unclassified information. Pasting a constituent's record into a public chatbot is a reportable data spill.

## Data Categories

- **Public data** (for example, published reports and open data from data.gov) may be used with any approved tool.
- **Internal data** may be used only with the agency-provisioned enterprise assistant operating under the agency's data-protection agreement.
- **Sensitive or regulated data** (PII, health, financial, law enforcement) may be used only with tools specifically authorized for that category.

## Accountability

Employees remain responsible for the accuracy of any AI-assisted work product. AI tools may produce confident but incorrect statements ("hallucinations"); factual claims must be verified against authoritative sources before release. Suspected misuse or data exposure must be reported to the information security office.
