# Lab 3.1 — Output rubric

Score **each of the four outputs** on all three dimensions. 3 points each, 9 max
per task.

| | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accurate?** | Contains a claim the source does not support | Nothing wrong, but hedged or vague where the source is specific | Every claim traces to the source material |
| **Complete?** | Misses a point you would have to add before sending | Covers the substance, thin in one place | Covers what a reader needs, nothing important left out |
| **Right format / tone?** | Ignored the format or the audience | Right shape, wrong register (too technical, too casual) | Exactly the format asked for, pitched at the stated reader |

## Using it

Score fast — this is a 35-minute lab and you have four tasks. First instinct is
usually right.

Post your **best** and your **worst** output to the Mural board with its score.
The spread across the room is the point of the debrief: the same prompt against
different assistants does not land in the same place.
