MEMORANDUM

TO:       Division Directors
FROM:     Office of the Chief Data Officer
DATE:     March 14, 2026
SUBJECT:  Interim Guidance on Generative AI for Constituent Services

1. PURPOSE. This memorandum provides interim guidance for the responsible use of
generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools in support of constituent services,
pending issuance of the Department's final policy.

2. BACKGROUND. Divisions have begun piloting generative AI assistants to draft
correspondence, summarize public records, and triage incoming requests. Early
results show meaningful time savings in routine drafting tasks. At the same time,
pilots have surfaced two recurring risks: the entry of nonpublic constituent
information into tools not approved for that data, and the release of AI-drafted
material that was not reviewed for accuracy.

3. GUIDANCE.
   a. Human review. Every AI-assisted work product intended for a constituent or
      for the public record must be reviewed and approved by a responsible employee
      before release. The employee, not the tool, is accountable for accuracy.
   b. Data handling. Only public information may be entered into public AI tools.
      Constituent records, Social Security numbers, and other personally
      identifiable information must never be entered into a tool that has not been
      approved for that data category. Suspected exposure must be reported within
      one business day.
   c. Approved tools. Divisions must use the enterprise assistant provisioned under
      the Department's data-protection agreement for any task involving internal
      information. A current list of approved tools is maintained by the Office of
      the Chief Information Officer.
   d. Recordkeeping. AI-assisted correspondence that documents agency business is a
      federal record and must be retained according to the applicable records
      schedule.

4. EFFECTIVE DATE. This guidance is effective immediately and remains in effect
until superseded by final Department policy.

5. POINT OF CONTACT. Questions may be directed to the Office of the Chief Data
Officer.
