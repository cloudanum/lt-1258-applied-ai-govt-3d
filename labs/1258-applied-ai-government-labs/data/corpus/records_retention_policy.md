# Records Retention Policy (Training Excerpt)

*Simplified, synthetic training document modeled on public federal records-management practice. Course labs only.*

## Purpose

This policy establishes how long agency records are kept and when they are destroyed or transferred to the National Archives. It implements the agency's records schedule approved by NARA.

## Record Categories and Retention Periods

- **Routine administrative correspondence** is retained for 3 years, then destroyed.
- **Program case files** are retained for 7 years after the case is closed.
- **Financial and procurement records** are retained for 6 years and 3 months in accordance with federal appropriations requirements.
- **Permanent records** of historical value are transferred to the National Archives 15 years after creation.

## Electronic Records

Electronic records are subject to the same retention periods as their paper equivalents. Email that documents agency business is a federal record and must be retained according to its content, not its format. Automatic deletion rules must not remove email before its retention period ends.

## Legal Holds

When litigation is reasonably anticipated, the destruction of potentially relevant records is suspended under a legal hold. The hold overrides the normal retention schedule until it is lifted by the Office of General Counsel.

## Destruction

Records that have met their retention period are destroyed using methods appropriate to their sensitivity. Records containing personally identifiable information are shredded or securely wiped. A destruction log records what was destroyed and when.
