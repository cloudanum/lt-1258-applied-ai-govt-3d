# Artificial Intelligence: Agencies Have Begun Adopting Generative AI but Need Stronger Oversight of Constituent-Facing Uses

**Report to Congressional Requesters — GAO-STYLE-XX-000000 (synthetic)**

## Why We Did This Study

Federal agencies increasingly use generative AI to draft correspondence,
summarize public records, and triage constituent requests. Agencies reported 412
such use cases in the most recent inventory, up from 173 the year before. We were
asked to review how agencies govern these uses, whether they measure results, and
what safeguards apply when a constituent is on the receiving end.

## What We Found

Agencies have moved faster than their governance. Of the 24 agencies reviewed,
21 had at least one generative AI pilot supporting constituent services, but only
9 had issued written guidance covering what data staff may enter into those
tools. Seven agencies allowed staff to use publicly available assistants for
official work without a data-protection agreement in place.

Measurement is thin. Sixteen agencies described time savings in routine drafting,
but only 4 could produce a baseline they measured against. None of the 24 tracked
whether AI-assisted correspondence required more or fewer follow-up contacts from
the constituent — the outcome that would show whether quality held.

Human review is uneven. All 21 agencies with pilots told us a person reviews AI
output before release. In practice, 6 agencies applied that rule only to
correspondence signed by senior officials, leaving routine replies unreviewed.
Two agencies could not say who was accountable when an AI-drafted reply contained
an error.

Records practices lag as well. Nine agencies had not determined whether
AI-assisted correspondence constitutes a federal record, and three could not
retrieve the prompts that produced a released document.

## What We Recommend

We are making 11 recommendations, including that agencies (1) issue written
guidance on permitted data categories before expanding constituent-facing pilots,
(2) establish a measurable baseline for each use case before deployment, (3)
apply human review to all constituent-facing output rather than by seniority, and
(4) determine the records status of AI-assisted correspondence. The agencies
concurred with 10 recommendations and partially concurred with 1.
